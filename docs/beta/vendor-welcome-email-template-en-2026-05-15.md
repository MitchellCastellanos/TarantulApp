### One thing pending: your **"Verified Shop"** badge

The badge isn't given out for paying — it's **earned**. It's the trust signal buyers see before messaging you. For the team to activate it, **reply to this email** with the following:

**Required:**
1. **Government-issued ID** (front and back of INE or equivalent). Reviewed only, not archived long-term.
2. **1–2 photos of the space** where you keep the animals (rack, room, current enclosures).
3. **3–5 inventory photos** — in each photo, include a handwritten note with your TarantulApp `@handle` visible (proves the photos are yours, not borrowed from another breeder).
4. **WhatsApp or Instagram** with at least 2–3 months of activity tied to your shop/breeding work.
5. **If you sell CITES species** (Poecilotheria metallica/regalis/ornata/rufilata/etc.): UMA number or CITES origin permit reference.

**Optional (speeds up the review):**
6. RFC / tax ID with related business activity.
7. 1–2 references from recognized keepers/breeders in the community.
8. If you sell supplies: invoice from a wholesale substrate / live food supplier.

### Why we ask for this

The badge protects community trust. Buyers are taking a real risk buying live animals from strangers online — the badge tells them **"this person exists, has real space, has their own inventory, isn't a reseller with stolen photos"**. It protects you too: serious shops stand out; resellers get filtered.

Review is human (we do it), usually **24–72 hours** after receiving your reply.

While the badge is pending, you can already publish everything. Without the badge, your storefront shows as **"New shop"** until verification clears.
